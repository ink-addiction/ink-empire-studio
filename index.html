Getting Inked: Before & After

💪 Before Your Session

  • Hydrate well. Hydrated skin takes ink more easily and heals faster.
  • Eat a solid meal 1–2 hours before to keep blood sugar steady.
  • Skip alcohol for 24 hours. It thins the blood and affects the result.
  • Sleep well and wear loose, comfortable clothing.
  • Bring water, a snack and headphones for longer sittings.

🩹 Aftercare Essentials

  • Keep the wrap on as instructed, then wash gently with lukewarm water and fragrance-free soap. Pat dry, never rub.
  • Moisturize lightly 2–3 times a day with unscented lotion.
  • Don't pick or scratch. Peeling and itching around days 5–14 is normal.
  • No pools, hot tubs or soaking for 2–3 weeks.
  • Protect from sun while healing, then use SPF 30+ to keep the ink vivid.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it hurt?

A little, but usually far less than first-timers fear. Most people describe a scratching or vibrating sensation, and the body adapts after the first few minutes. Forearms, calves and shoulders are the easiest places to start.

How much will my tattoo cost?

Pricing depends on size, detail and placement. Send your idea and placement for a personal estimate.

How long does a tattoo take to heal?

The surface heals in about 2–4 weeks; full healing under the skin can take up to six months. Light scabbing and peeling in week one or two is normal.

Can you fix or cover an old tattoo?

In most cases, yes. Cover-ups start with an assessment of your existing piece and an honest look at what's possible.
